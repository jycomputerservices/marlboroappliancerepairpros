Signs your dryer heat system is failing

Heat problems can show up as cold tumbling, weak heat, short heating bursts, or longer dry times.

The dryer tumbles but clothes stay damp or cold
Heat starts and then stops before the load is dry
The dryer runs hotter than usual or shuts off mid-cycle

Parts and Systems We Check

Dryer heat diagnosis checks airflow and safety devices before replacing heating parts.

Thermal fuse and thermostats

Safety fuses and cycling thermostats can open when airflow or heat control fails.

Heating element or igniter

Electric elements and gas igniters are common no-heat parts.

Gas valve coils or control board

Gas dryers may ignite briefly and then stop when coils or controls fail.

Risk

Potential Hazards

Dryer heat issues can overlap with fire and gas safety concerns.

Lint and airflow risk

Restricted airflow can overheat the dryer and trip safety devices.

Gas ignition concerns

Delayed ignition, gas odor, or failed burner operation should be treated urgently.

Overheating

Bypassed or ignored safety devices can create dangerous temperatures.

Cost Impact

Financial Impact if You Wait

Dryer heat problems can waste energy and damage clothing if ignored.

Higher utility use

Running multiple cycles increases gas or electric costs.

Clothing wear

Excessive cycles and overheating can shrink or damage fabrics.

Part escalation

Airflow problems can repeatedly blow fuses until the underlying cause is fixed.
