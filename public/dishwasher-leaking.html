How dishwasher leaks show up

A dishwasher may leak only during fill, wash, drain, or when spray pressure hits the door.

Water appears at the front corners or under the door
The leak happens only during part of the cycle
Cabinets or flooring near the dishwasher feel damp
View Likely Causes

Local diagnostic note. Stop running the dishwasher until the source is found; repeated small leaks can reach cabinets and subflooring.

Likely Causes

Parts and Systems We Check

Leak diagnosis checks door sealing, water level, spray pattern, and under-unit components.

Door gasket and latch

A worn gasket or weak latch can let spray escape at the front.

Float switch and inlet valve

Overfilling can happen when fill controls do not stop water properly.

Pump seals, hoses, and clamps

Leaks underneath often come from pump assemblies, hoses, or loose connections.

Risk

Potential Hazards

Dishwasher leaks can hide under cabinets where damage grows quietly.

Electrical and water proximity

Dishwashers combine motors, wiring, and water in a confined space.

Cabinet moisture

Leaks can swell cabinet bases and toe kicks.

Slip hazard

Water spreading across kitchen flooring can create a fall risk.

Cost Impact

Financial Impact if You Wait

The repair cost is often smaller than the surrounding water damage.

Cabinet replacement risk

Pressed cabinet bases can swell or delaminate after repeated leaks.

Flooring damage

Water can get below tile, vinyl, or wood before it is visible.

More part failures

A leak can damage pumps, motors, or controls if water reaches them.
