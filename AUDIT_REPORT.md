# SEO & UX Audit Report
## marlboroappliancerepairpros.com → Central NJ Appliance Experts

**Date:** May 2026  
**Current Status:** Functional lead-gen site with strong foundation, significant optimization opportunities  

---

## PHASE 1: AUDIT FINDINGS

### A. CURRENT STRENGTHS

**Domain & SEO Equity**
- Keep existing domain (marlboroappliancerepairpros.com) - retains all link equity
- Good domain age and history
- Main navigation covers key service pages

**Conversion Elements**
- Multiple CTAs visible above fold
- Lead form collects: Name, Phone, Appliance Type, ZIP Code, Issue Description
- Click-to-call buttons prominent
- Trust badges displayed (Licensed & Insured, 90-day warranty, 24/7 emergency)
- Service process clearly explained (Call → Diagnosis → Repair)

**User Experience**
- Clean, professional design
- Mobile-responsive layout
- Dark navy/white color scheme works well
- Good visual hierarchy
- Clear value proposition

**Content**
- Service pages exist (Refrigerator, Washer, Dryer, Oven/Stove, Dishwasher)
- Footer lists nearby service areas (good localization signal)
- 24/7 messaging emphasizes emergency service

---

### B. CRITICAL ISSUES

**1. Broken Links & Missing Pages**
- ⚠️ SERVICE AREA PAGE BROKEN (404)
  - Linked in main nav but returns "Page Not Found"
  - Footer lists 9 towns but no dedicated pages exist
  - HUGE SEO opportunity being wasted

**2. Phone Number Inconsistencies**
- Homepage shows: (733) 455-0102
- Service pages show: (732) 555-0101
- Creates confusion and may impact local SEO
- **ACTION:** Determine single authoritative number

**3. Thin Content**
- Service pages have minimal unique content
- No detailed service descriptions
- Missing keyword targets
- No problem-specific content ("Dryer Not Heating", "Fridge Leaking", etc.)

**4. Missing SEO Elements**
- No visible FAQ section
- No schema markup visible (no LocalBusiness, FAQPage, Service)
- No breadcrumb navigation
- Form has generic placeholder text
- Missing customer testimonials/social proof
- No internal linking strategy visible

**5. Brand Issues**
- Current brand: "Marlboro Appliance Repair Pros" (generic, local-only feeling)
- Needs rebranding to: "Central NJ Appliance Experts" (regional, trusted feeling)
- Current visual branding is fine; copy/messaging needs update

**6. Conversion Rate Issues**
- Form has placeholder text ("Other Appliance", example text) - confusing UX
- No sticky mobile CTA for mobile users
- Lead form not optimized for quick form completion
- Missing urgency messaging on service pages
- No pricing/cost information
- Missing customer testimonials or reviews
