Likely Causes

Parts and Systems We Check

Dishwasher drain problems can be inside the appliance or at the kitchen sink connection.

Drain pump

A failing pump may hum, click, or fail to move water out of the tub.

Filter, hose, or check valve

Food debris, glass, labels, and grease can restrict the drain path.

Air gap or disposal connection

A blocked air gap or improper disposal connection can send water back toward the dishwasher.

Risk

Potential Hazards

Standing dishwasher water can create odor, overflow, and sanitation concerns.

Dirty water exposure

Standing water may contain food debris and detergent residue.

Overflow risk

A blocked drain can leak through the door or underside during later cycles.

Kitchen moisture

Repeated leaks can affect cabinets, flooring, and toe-kick areas.

Cost Impact

Financial Impact if You Wait

Drain issues can be inexpensive when caught early but costly after kitchen damage.

Cabinet and floor damage

Water under the dishwasher can spread before it is visible.

Repeated failed cycles

Running extra cycles wastes water and detergent without solving the blockage.

Pump damage

Debris in the drain path can damage the pump impeller or motor.
