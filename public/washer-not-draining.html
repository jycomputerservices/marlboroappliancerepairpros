Signs the washer drain system is blocked or failing

Drain problems can appear at the end of a cycle or stop the washer before it reaches spin.

Water remains in the drum after the cycle ends
The washer hums, clicks, or stops before draining
Drain or OE/F21-style error codes appear on the display
View Likely Causes

Local diagnostic note. If the washer is full of water, avoid forcing the door open on a front-loader; that can release several gallons onto the floor.

Likely Causes

Parts and Systems We Check

The drain path includes several parts that can clog, kink, fail electrically, or report the wrong water level.

Drain pump

A worn or jammed pump may hum without moving water or fail intermittently under load.

Drain hose and filter

Coins, lint, small garments, and debris can block the filter, hose, or standpipe connection.

Pressure switch or water level sensor

If the washer thinks water is still present, it may pause or refuse to spin.

Risk

Potential Hazards

Standing water can become a leak risk and can also make the washer unsafe to move or open.

Sudden water release

Opening the door or disconnecting hoses without draining can flood the laundry area.

Odor and residue

Stagnant water can create odor and residue inside the tub and boot seal.

Pump overheating

A jammed pump can overheat if the washer keeps attempting to drain.

Cost Impact

Financial Impact if You Wait

Drain problems are often repairable, but waiting can add cleanup and part costs.

Floor and cabinet damage

One release of trapped water can damage nearby flooring, trim, or cabinetry.

Repeated cycle costs

Running extra cycles wastes water, electricity, and time without clearing the problem.

Pump and control strain

A blocked drain can stress the pump and sometimes the control system.
